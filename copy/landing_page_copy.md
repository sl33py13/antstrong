- Step 1 (Local Ingestion): Drop operational datasets into your local antSTRONG folder. Our built-in normalizer parses non-standard date formats, currency strings, and missing fields automatically.
- Step 2 (LocalKeyVault PII Scrubbing): Before any analysis runs, the local scrubber replaces client names, emails, phone numbers, and internal IPs with unique vault tokens (CLIENT_014, TECH_03, IP_009). The lookup key stays strictly inside your local vault.
